Tab 03 · The marketing closed loop

Measure honestly at small n. Decide before the data begs.

At 23 deals, multi-touch attribution is statistical noise wearing a dashboard. The loop below trades false precision for honest signal: one required attribution question, an account-level engagement score, a coverage ratio that warns a year early, and kill and double-down rules signed before campaigns launch — so every verdict is a reading of evidence, not a negotiation with sunk cost.

3a · The measurement stack — three instruments, no attribution theatre

Self-reported attribution

A single required field at every hand-raise: "what prompted you to talk to us?" Free text, read by a human. Unreasonably effective in long trust cycles — and the only attribution that survives small n.

Account engagement score

Scored per account across the 5–9 person buying group, not per lead — depth (who) × recency × deliberateness (what they did). This is what moves an account to S2 and keeps stage calls honest.

Coverage ratio — the early warning

Open qualified pipeline ÷ remaining pillar target. Watched per pillar, monthly. Coverage degrades a year before revenue does — it is the instrument that says which pillar misses FY2028 while there is still time to act.

3b · Cadence — who reads what, when, and who decides

Weekly · 15 min · solo

Trigger scan, engagement moves, plays in flight. Agentic workflows compile; the human scans for anomalies.

Monthly · Pipeline Council · 60 min

Marketing + CCO + sales. Stage movement by pillar, coverage ratio, play performance, handoff friction. Decisions inside pillars are made here.

Quarterly · half-day · CCO + CEO

Kill / double-down verdicts against the pre-committed rules; budget re-vote across pillars; the FY2028 split treated as a standing hypothesis to falsify.

Automation compiles and flags; it never kills a channel, moves budget or re-messages a persona on its own. That judgement is the job.

3c · Pre-committed kill rules

  • Zero engaged accounts, two quarters → the play is killed. There is no third quarter.
  • Cost per engaged account >3× blended, two quarters → killed or rebuilt from scratch — not "optimised".
  • P4 anchor-partner gate missed (end Q2 FY2027) → ANZ budget frozen and transferred to P3.
  • Developer PLG <50 activations/quarter, two quarters → the experiment ends.

Exempt from kill rules

Regulator proximity and reference manufacturing — trust infrastructure on multi-year clocks, reviewed annually instead.

3d · Pre-committed double-down rules

  • Cost per engaged account under half blended, two quarters → budget +50% the following quarter.
  • A research asset drives >15% of a quarter's engaged accounts → it becomes a series.
  • A named reference lands → the reference programme is funded immediately; nothing converts regulated buyers faster.
  • A pillar runs >20% over coverage, two quarters → budget reallocates to it from the weakest pillar.

Symmetry matters: pre-committing the upside rules stops winners being starved by inertia just as the kill rules stop losers being fed by hope.
